Privacy Policy — Unpick

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Unpick is a cryptic crossword game. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information whatsoever. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What the app stores

Unpick saves the following on your device only, using your browser's local storage:

  • the name you optionally type on the welcome screen;
  • your streak, solved count, scores and which clues you have seen;
  • your theme and difficulty preferences.

None of this ever leaves your phone. It is not backed up to us, because there is no "us" to back it up to — the app has no server. Uninstalling the app deletes it, and the Reset button inside the app clears it at any time.

Network use

The app requests the INTERNET permission for one purpose: to download three typefaces (Fraunces, Archivo and JetBrains Mono) from Google Fonts the first time it runs, after which they are cached. Google may log the IP address making that request, as it would for any website; see Google's own privacy policy. If your device is offline the app works normally and simply uses a system typeface instead. Every clue is stored inside the app, so no puzzle content is ever fetched.

Sharing

If you tap a share or report button, the app hands text to Android's share sheet and you choose where it goes. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you send it yourself.

Children

Unpick is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
